## Description

Priya says her work laptop will not turn on when it is sitting in the docking station at her desk. There are no lights on the laptop and the dock's power light is off. She was working normally yesterday afternoon.

## Troubleshooting steps

1. Asked Priya to check the dock's power cable at the wall and at the dock: both firmly seated. Dock power light stays off.
2. Moved the dock's power adapter to a different wall socket (checked with a desk lamp: the socket works). No change.
3. Asked her to unplug the laptop from the dock and plug in its own charger directly. The charging light on the laptop comes on and the laptop starts normally.
4. Docked the laptop again with the dock plugged into the working socket: still no dock light.
5. Priya notices the dock's power adapter is warm to the touch and has a faint burnt smell.
