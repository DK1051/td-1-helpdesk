## Description

## Troubleshooting steps

3. Hannah connected the laptop to her phone's hotspot instead of home Wi-Fi: the VPN connects within seconds.
4. Back on home Wi-Fi, checked the laptop's date and time: correct. The VPN client is one version behind the current release.
5. Her internet provider's (ISP's) router was replaced last weekend, according to Hannah. Not yet checked: router settings that might block VPN traffic.
