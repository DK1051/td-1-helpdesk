## Description

Marcus has been given a company-managed phone for branch-manager duties. When he installs the company app and signs in to enrol the phone, the app stops at "This device does not meet your organisation's requirements" and will not continue. He is enrolling the phone through the normal company process with his manager's approval already on file.

## Troubleshooting steps

1. Confirmed the company app version in the app store listing is current and installed on the phone.
2. Marcus read out the phone's operating system version from Settings: it is two major versions older than the minimum listed in the device policy.
3. Checked the phone has 30% battery and enough free storage for a system update. It is on the office Wi-Fi.
4. Marcus has not yet run the system update. Phone has not been added to the company device list.
